Digital instalment journey · Germany pilot

Now / Next / Later roadmap

Sequenced for the shift-working consumer who must commit to a plan she trusts, without calling anyone. Value is scored 1–5 against Self-Service Rate only.

Applied constraints

Team 1 designer, 1 subject-matter expert, 6 developers (3 senior, 1 mid-level, 1 junior), 2 QA engineers.
What this team can build Frontend and middleware only. Backend and API changes must be requested from the broader organisation. Developers may occasionally support another team’s domain.
Cadence and scoring rule Sprints are 2 weeks. Now is the next 3 sprints. Next is the 3 after that. A feature only scores high if it moves SSR for this persona.
Persona Shift worker, income varies week to week, one or more open claims, resolving them in the portal.
Primary metric Self-Service Rate: resolved, with no inbound contact in the 30 days before or after.
Moment of misery The commit screen shows one number with no reason, and no explanation of a missed payment. She either calls, stalls, or agrees and later defaults.

Now

In-team. No backend dependency. Hits fear, consequence, and the call she makes just to understand.
