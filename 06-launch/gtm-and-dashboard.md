# GTM Launch Plan, Riverty business-unit case

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Missed-payment explainer |
| Goal | Conversion |
| Launch tier | M, Targeted |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Conversion, It addresses one of the major pain point for a significant portion of users: lack of confidence and trust in the proposed instalment plan and consequences of a missed payment.
- **Target audience:** Shift worker, income varies week to week, one or more open claims, resolving them in the portal.

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, As the feature will be a small but mandatory step in the instalment plan flow, there is no need to draw consumer attention to this: Every consumer for whom this is relevant will see it. This creates a "controlled environment" that also enables us to measure the impact of the chance: does it actually move the needle on the conversion from started to finisihed instalment plan.
I recommend, however, informing our clients (i.e. not the end-user/consumer) about the change to take the opportunity to advertise our data-driven approach and to make them aware that consumers (i.e. their customers) may reach out to them about the change.

## Channels
1. **Owned: E-Mail to clients to inform about change**
2. **Owned: Addition to slide deck for sales to advertise how this feature is incorporated into our consumer journey**
3. **Owned: Case Study on own website to showcase data-driven development and focus on consumer insights at riverty. Hammer down the message taht fairness towards counsumers is a key behavioural anchor for us.**

## Enablement & Assets
Slides for sales, text bricks for customer service, live demo internally, access for all stakeholders to a demo or actual test system.

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** PM owns GTM plan, Key Account Managment owns outreach to clients. Customer Success Management owns messages provided to clients (to prepare them for consumer questions), Marketing owns Website update and case study.
- **Timeline:** Week 1: Outreach to Clients (2-week warning) and textbricks, 
Week 3 + 4: phaed rollout to different markets or segments to validate that nothing breaks.
Week 5 and 6: measure results
after week 6: incorporate case study on website and in slide deck.

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Positive Consumer feedback
Stable usage and funnel entry
improved funnel performance
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Consumers find the feature confusing and it acturally hurts the conversion
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Iterate on UX to further improve effect.
