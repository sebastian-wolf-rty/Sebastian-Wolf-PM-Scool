- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** Riverty Case Study: BU Collection — A Fair Way Forward

- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must solve payment default risk, high cost per claim and damage to Riverty reputation by giving consumers agency of resolving their claims independently and reliably.

Ongoing data-driven analysis of funnel adoption provide the chance to tweak the instalment funnel to perfection.
